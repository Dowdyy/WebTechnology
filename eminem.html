01 / ABOUT THE ARTIST

Music that touches the soul

Eminem, also known as Marshall Bruce Mathers, is an american rapper, songwriter and producer. He is mainly known for his rap flow and how fast he can rap. Eminem is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influientioal rappers of all time.

During his younger years Eminem we frequently moved between Missouri and Michigan. Due to this he attended up to 30 seperate, making him a primary target for bullies. Meaning a lot of his earlier songs are about him processing his trauma and telling his story with a little dramatic flair.

Listen For

Listening to Eminem can be like a roller coaster, one minuet your jumping up and down and feel like you can take the world on. Next your in your seat gripping it as everything starts to turn sad and angry. Eminem is one of the few rappers in the world that is able to make the listener go through all the emotions he is feeling as you listen to him.
