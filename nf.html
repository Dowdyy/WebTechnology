01 / ABOUT THE ARTIST

Music that touches the soul

NF is an amarican rapper/songwriter that is known for creating music that has some personal reflection and emotional honesty throughout all of his music.

Throughout all of his songs he returns to themes like pressure, self-doubt, ambition and working though the hard times. While also having a cinematic feel to the videos but still keeping the focus on the lyrics at all times.

Listen For

Listen to how as he gets more pent up with feelings the enviroment changes and everything is more dramatic so much you can feel the lyrics in your soul as hes singing them.
